Post to TikTok

About this tool: Xem Một Lát lets a creator post their own short vertical videos to their own TikTok account. Log in with TikTok, review the video and caption, choose who can view it, then press Post. The video is posted only to the TikTok account you logged in with. The owner's videos are AI-generated stories/clips and are labeled as AI-generated on TikTok.

Step 1 – Connect your TikTok account

Log in with TikTok and authorize the scopes user.info.basic, video.publish, video.upload.

Step 2 – Post to TikTok
